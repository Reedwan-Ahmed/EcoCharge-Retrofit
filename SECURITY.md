# Safety and vulnerability reporting

EcoCharge interfaces with mains electricity and a high-current battery system. Incorrect implementation can cause electric shock, fire, battery damage, or injury.

- Do not connect this prototype to a vehicle or public electrical supply based only on this repository.
- Do not enable firmware outputs without a reviewed schematic, protection design, isolation verification, calibration, and staged test procedure.
- Do not disclose exploitable electrical or firmware weaknesses publicly before maintainers assess them.

For safety/security issues, contact maintainers through a private channel agreed by the team. No public contact address was supplied.
