# 7. Proposed 12-Month Roadmap

These phases reproduce the roadmap on page 11 of the supplied presentation. They are proposed milestones, not completed work.

| Period | Proposed milestone | Evidence / exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1–2 | Bench prototype | Reviewed schematic, PCB/enclosure, protection review, instrumented low-risk bring-up, thermal checks |
| Month 3 | Battery-set test | Approved charge profile, repeated CC-CV logs, termination validation, battery temperature/capacity tracking |
| Months 4–6 | A/B pilot on 10 vehicles | Matched control group, baseline and retrofit energy meter data, safety logs, feedback |
| Months 9–12 | Install 500 boxes | Pilot review, manufacturing QA, service process, warranty/incident process |

## Suggested A/B pilot

1. Recruit comparable vehicles, packs, chargers, usage patterns, and charging locations.
2. Meter energy at the same boundary for baseline and intervention periods.
3. Record time-of-day, kWh, battery voltage/current/temperature, pack age, and ambient conditions.
4. Track battery performance using repeatable capacity/service tests, not recollection alone.
5. Record failures, nuisance trips, overheating, interruptions, and maintenance.
6. Predefine primary outcomes and report uncertainty.
7. Obtain informed consent and follow electrical / field-test safety requirements.

Do not claim a saving percentage until the A/B dataset, metering method, and analysis are published.
